Financial Markets & Derivatives Research

Indian equity-index derivatives · Nifty 50 & Bank Nifty

My independent market research has involved Indian equity-index derivatives, options strategies, market behaviour, probability, position sizing and risk-management concepts. It has helped me examine how uncertainty, leverage, volatility and decision-making affect financial outcomes.

  • Backtested intraday options strategies on Stockmock, including ratio spreads and multi-leg short strangles (e.g. 3 OTM calls and 3 OTM puts sold), with defined ranges and adjustment rules.
  • Structured wide-range credit ratio spreads against my own market view: for a sideways-to-bearish view, a call ratio spread kept the net credit if the market fell, leaving only the upside to manage.
  • Tracked intraday ranges and option-chain behaviour using Option-i-Chart and InstaOptions alongside TradingView.
  • Swing-traded equities by combining price-action analysis with balance-sheet, income-statement and cash-flow analysis.
  • Traded live with personal capital and ultimately lost the account. Studying why showed me how position size, leverage and behaviour after losses compound, and how I personally respond to risk.

This was personal trading and research, not employment or fund management. Backtests are simulations, not live results, and no performance figures are claimed.

Portfolio Study: ETF Core, Stock Satellite

  • Studied asset allocation, diversification, rebalancing and risk management, using the publicly described Yale Endowment approach as learning material (no affiliation).
  • Planned a framework with an index-ETF core: low cost, no lock-in, broad exposure to the largest companies and simple rebalancing.

  • A debt sleeve is kept as dry powder to rebalance into the same ETFs after large falls.
  • Designed around investor behaviour: a stable core reduces the urge to panic-sell in drawdowns.
